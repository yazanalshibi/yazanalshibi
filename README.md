# Market Twin

AI-native **Market Intelligence & Consumer Simulation Platform**.

The platform builds a living, machine-readable representation of a market — a **Market Twin** — so businesses can understand what exists, what consumers need, how they behave, what they pay, and how they may respond when an offer changes.

> **Principle:** First understand the market from evidence. Then simulate possibilities. Then validate against reality.

## Capability loop

`UNDERSTAND → DISCOVER → SIMULATE → TEST → OPTIMIZE → VALIDATE → LEARN`

## Initial focus

| Dimension | Choice |
|-----------|--------|
| Geography | Greater Toronto Area (GTA) |
| Seed vertical | Automotive services (development environment, not product identity) |
| Modes | **IDEA → MARKET** (test a concept) and later **MARKET → IDEA** (discover opportunities) |
